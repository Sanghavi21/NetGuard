How this measurement works: NetGuard samples Windows network adapter counters every second while this page is open and saves the measured byte deltas locally. The first sample establishes a baseline; collection starts on the next sample. Adapter counters cannot identify individual applications, and VPN/virtual adapters can mirror traffic from another adapter, so use one adapter at a time. Daily and monthly totals reflect the time NetGuard has been monitoring, not usage from before monitoring started.
